# Legal and Safety Notes

## Product positioning

RaceNavi AI provides:
- prediction information
- bet simulation
- learning/review support

RaceNavi AI does not provide:
- automatic betting
- purchase代行
- guaranteed profit
- guaranteed hit rate

## Required disclaimer

Use this text near bet plan outputs:

```text
この提案は予想情報・買い目シミュレーションであり、的中や利益を保証するものではありません。馬券の購入は必ずご自身の判断と責任の範囲で行ってください。
```

## Data use

For MVP:
- Use mock data only.

For production:
- Confirm rights and terms for JRA-VAN or other official/legal data sources.
- Avoid unauthorized scraping.
- Store only necessary user data.
- If analytics are used, anonymize and disclose in privacy policy.

## UI restrictions

Avoid words:
- 絶対
- 必勝
- 確実
- 儲かる
- 回収率保証
